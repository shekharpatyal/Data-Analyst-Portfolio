## 📊 Projects

### 1. Customer Churn Analysis (Python)
Analyzed 7,043 telecom customers to understand why they leave and how to retain them.

**Key Findings:**
- 26.5% overall churn rate
- Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7% vs 2.8% for 2-year contracts
- New customers (0-12 months) have 47.4% churn rate
- Fiber optic users churn at 41.9%

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

[View Project](./01-Python-Customer-Churn/)

### 2. Music Store Analysis (SQL)
Analyzed digital music store data using advanced SQL queries.

**Key Findings:**
- Rock genre accounts for 835 track sales
- Jane Peacock generates 35.77% of total sales
- Top customer: Helena Holý ($49.62 lifetime value)
- Built customer lifetime value segmentation using NTILE()

**Concepts:** JOINs, CTEs, Window Functions (RANK, LAG, NTILE)

[View Project](./02-SQL-Music-Store-Analysis/)

### 3. HR Analytics Dashboard (SQL + Tableau)
Analyzed employee attrition for 1,470 employees to identify patterns and reduce turnover.

**Key Findings:**
- Sales Department has highest attrition at 20.6%
- Under 25 age group has 39.2% attrition (highest risk)
- Sales Representative role has 39.8% attrition
- Overall attrition rate: 16.1%

**Tools:** SQL, Tableau

### 4. Airbnb Market Analysis (Tableau)
Analyzed Airbnb listings data for pricing trends and market patterns.

**Key Findings:**
- 6-bedroom properties command highest avg price at $584.8/night
- 1-bedroom listings dominate with 1,811 properties (market majority)
- Revenue shows consistent upward trend throughout 2016
- Zipcode-level analysis reveals significant price variations

**Tools:** Tableau
